What it is for

Two models, one set of running gear

Xiaojingling 小精灵 and Xiaoxuanfeng 小旋风 share everything that moves: a 12-tube controller on a 500 W motor, a split 130 rear axle, hydraulic suspension with a 31 damper, and 300-12 tyres front and rear. The difference between them is the body, which is why we quote them as a pair rather than making you choose between them.

This is the body for work where the route is tighter than the load — workshop and yard movement, market rows and narrow lanes, smallholder plots, and the last stretch of a delivery. The 850 mm width is the point of it: it goes where a wider cargo deck cannot.

If your loads are heavy, or your roads are unsealed, this is the wrong family. Step up to the cargo flatbed instead — a longer deck and a heavier axle cost less over a season than replacing a light axle twice.

How to choose

Three things decide it

Narrow access

If the route is the constraint — alleyways, market rows, yard doorways — the 1100 mm body is the one that fits. A 1600 mm deck is not an upgrade if it cannot reach the shop.

Sealed, level going

The 500 W drive and 300-12 tyres suit hard, level surfaces and short runs. On broken or steep ground the same body works harder and lasts less.

Fleet simplicity

Because the two models share their running gear, spares are common across the pair. For a small fleet, that is worth more than the body shape.
