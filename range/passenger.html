What it is for

Three bodies, three different jobs

The Open Rickshaw (2900 × 1700 mm, 1000 W differential drive) carries four passengers under a rigid canopy — the body for licensed urban transport and tourism. The Double-Row 2-Seat is an 805 mm wide body for lanes, campuses and yards where a full-width tricycle does not fit. The Tuk-Tuk 6–9 Seat is the three-row taxi body at 3300 mm long, on 1500 W to 2500 W.
