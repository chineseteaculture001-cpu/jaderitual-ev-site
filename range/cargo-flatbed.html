What it is for

Six models from three deck sizes

Jingfeng 劲风, Chifeng 驰风, M2, Hanfeng 悍风, Mengqin 猛禽 and Zunguan 尊冠. The family spans three deck sizes — 1300 × 950, 1500 × 1000 and 1600 × 1100 mm, with 1800 × 1300 mm available on the three larger models — and the running gear climbs with them, from an integrated 160 axle on 350-12 / 375-12 tyres to a full-floating integrated 160 / 180 axle on 400-12 / 450-12 steel.

This is the body for moving goods as a business: building materials and hardware, agricultural produce, market and wholesale distribution, and rental fleets where one body has to do several jobs. Deck length, side-board height and power rating are specified together, so the same family covers a 1.3 m market run and a yard load on an 1.8 m deck.

Where two models read the same on paper, they are the same — the power and the running gear are what differ. Tell us the load and the road and we will quote the build that suits your route, not the one with the higher number.

How to choose

Three things decide it

Deck length follows the load

1300 mm for dense, stacked goods; 1500–1600 mm for mixed pallets and bags; 1800 mm when the load is long or bulky. A longer deck on the same axle adds overhang, not capacity.

Road surface sets the axle

Sealed urban routes are served by the 800 W builds on 375-12 tyres. Unsealed, rutted or graded roads want the full-floating 180 axle and 400-12 / 450-12 steel — that is what the extra money buys.

Power tracks the axle

800 W on an integrated 160; twin 1200 W on a full-floating 160 / 180; up to 1500 W on the 30-tube build. Buy the rating the route demands — the next size up costs range, not just money.
