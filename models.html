Built to order

What “built to order” actually means here

You are not choosing from a fixed catalogue. The body, the electrical set and the running gear are specified together, and the specification is written down before production starts.

Specification first

Deck size and side-board height, motor and controller rating, axle type, suspension and tyre set — agreed in writing before anything is built.

Sample, then container

Units can be supplied as a sample or trial order before you commit to a container, and consolidated with the spare parts that go with them.

Your market, your build

Voltage, charger and documentation are matched to the destination market, so the units that arrive are the units that can be registered and serviced there.
