01 — The Category

Why three wheels still win

最后一公里

Three-wheelers are not a compromise on four wheels. In the places they are used, they are the cheaper, nimbler and more serviceable answer — which is why demand keeps growing in the markets that matter to us.

01

Cost is per kilometre, not per unit

An electric drivetrain removes fuel from the running cost. What a fleet manager watches instead is battery life and how fast the unit earns back its purchase price.

02

Payload without the price tag

A cargo three-wheeler moves a load that is usefully close to a light pickup, at a fraction of the capital outlay — the reason fleets start here rather than with trucks.

03

It fits the streets you have

Narrow track and a tight turning circle let these units work in markets, alleys, yards and narrow farm tracks where a four-wheeler cannot turn or park.

04

Serviceable by local hands

When we specify, we favour designs a local workshop can maintain with standard components — not units that can only be repaired by the original factory.

05 — Compliance

Documents, certification and who does what

合规按目的国逐单确认

Certification for a vehicle depends on the destination market, not on the factory. We will tell you plainly what we can issue, what your market requires, and where the boundary of our responsibility sits — before you place an order, not after.

What we issue with every shipment

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, and certificate of origin. Where a manufacturer's declaration or test report exists for the model, it travels with the goods.

What depends on your market

Road registration, type approval and any import certification are governed by the destination country. Tell us the market at enquiry stage and we will state what is required.

Tariffs and duty

Import duty and any registration cost are the buyer's responsibility and vary widely by country — some markets tariff complete vehicles far more heavily than components.

No badge without a document

We do not print certification marks on a unit that does not carry the paperwork to support them. If a claim cannot be documented, we will not make it.
