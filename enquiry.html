Your enquiry

Nothing is submitted to this website or to any form service. Your enquiry is placed in your own WhatsApp or mail app with every field filled in, and you press send — which is also why this form does not attract automated spam.

What makes a quote possible

  • The body or bodies you are considering, and roughly how many units.
  • The destination country and port.
  • How the vehicles will be used: payload, road surface, daily distance.
  • Whether road registration is required, and any voltage or charger standard for your market.
  • Any OEM / ODM requirements — private label, colours, deck dimensions, packaging.

What happens next

  • Send this form through WhatsApp or your mail app.
  • We come back within one business day with questions or a specification to confirm.
  • You get a written quotation against that specification, and a sample or trial unit can be arranged before a container order.
